Snow World

A free physics-based sledding game. Race down the mountain, dodge obstacles, collect stars and lightning bolts, and set your best time on the global leaderboard.

How to play

Steer your sled using the on-screen controls or arrow keys, collect stars along the track, and avoid crashing to keep your run alive. Compare your score against other players on the leaderboard after each run.
